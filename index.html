Know Your Chef

Chef Arjun Malhotra

Founder & Executive Chef

With two decades spent mastering the ancient art of live-fire cooking across the kitchens of Tokyo, Copenhagen, and Mumbai, Chef Arjun created Saffron & Smoke as a sanctuary — a place where the primal dance of flame and the delicate whisper of saffron converge into something transcendent.

Every dish at Saffron & Smoke is a meditation on patience. We source our saffron from the high-altitude fields of Kashmir, our meats are aged in custom-built Himalayan salt chambers, and our smoking woods are hand-selected from ancient orchards. Nothing here is rushed. Nothing is ordinary.

"Cooking is not about ingredients. It is about the silence between the flames — the moment when heat transforms the ordinary into the unforgettable."
— Arjun Malhotra
